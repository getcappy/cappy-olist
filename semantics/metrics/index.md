# Metrics

29 metrics, grouped by business domain.

## Revenue & orders

* [GMV](gmv.md) - Gross merchandise value: item prices plus freight, in BRL.
* [Merchandise value](merchandise-value.md) - Item prices excluding freight, in BRL.
* [Freight value](freight-value.md) - Freight charged to customers, in BRL.
* [Orders](orders.md) - Number of orders placed.
* [Delivered orders](delivered-orders.md) - Orders with status delivered.
* [Canceled orders](canceled-orders.md) - Orders with status canceled.
* [Average order value (AOV)](average-order-value.md) - GMV divided by the number of orders.
* [Cancellation rate](cancellation-rate.md) - Canceled orders divided by all orders.

## Delivery performance

* [Late orders](late-orders.md) - Delivered orders that arrived after the estimated delivery date.
* [Late delivery rate](late-delivery-rate.md) - Late delivered orders divided by delivered orders.
* [Average delivery days](average-delivery-days.md) - Average days from purchase to delivery for delivered orders.

## Products & sellers

* [Items sold](items-sold.md) - Number of order items (units) sold.
* [Item revenue](item-revenue.md) - Item prices excluding freight.
* [Item freight](item-freight.md) - Freight on order items.
* [Average item price](average-item-price.md) - Item revenue divided by items sold.
* [Catalogue products](catalog-products.md) - Number of products in the catalogue.
* [Sellers](sellers.md) - Number of sellers.

## Customers

* [Customers](customers.md) - Number of distinct customers.
* [Repeat customers](repeat-customers.md) - Customers with more than one order.
* [Repeat customer rate](repeat-customer-rate.md) - Repeat customers divided by all customers.
* [Average customer value](average-customer-value.md) - Average lifetime GMV per customer.
* [Average orders per customer](average-orders-per-customer.md) - Average number of orders per customer.

## Payments

* [Payment value](payment-value.md) - Total value collected through payments.
* [Payment transactions](payment-transactions.md) - Number of payment transactions.
* [Average payment value](average-payment-value.md) - Payment value divided by payment transactions.

## Reviews

* [Average review score](average-review-score.md) - Average customer review score from 1 to 5.
* [Reviews](reviews.md) - Number of reviews.
* [Low reviews](low-reviews.md) - Reviews with a score of 1 or 2.
* [Low review rate](low-review-rate.md) - Low reviews divided by all reviews.
